- **Commit:** `32904bd`

**2. [Rule 1 - Bug] TomlReaderTests.swift + TOML fixtures used sk-or-FAKE_FIXTURE_KEY_XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX**

- **Fix:** Changed to `fake-or-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX` in all three files. Test assertions updated to match new value. TOML parse behavior unchanged.
- **Files modified:** `valid.toml`, `malformed.toml`, `TomlReaderTests.swift`
- **Commit:** `32904bd`

**3. [Rule 1 - Bug] ci.yml self-reference: the PATTERNS= line triggers its own grep scan**
